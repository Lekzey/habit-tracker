HabitTracker

What HabitTracker does

  • Tracks your time per site — split into Active (the tab you're actually looking at) and Background (open, but not the one in front).
  • Only the site name is stored (e.g. youtube.com) — never full URLs, page content, or anything you typed.
  • 100% local — everything stays on this device. No account, no server, no network requests, ever.

Around the app

  • Overview — totals, charts, and a full table for whatever date range you pick above.
  • Analytics → By site — click a site to see its day-by-day history and a yearly activity calendar.
  • Analytics → Categories — the same totals grouped into categories (News, AI, etc.) instead of by site; manage categories here too.
  • Settings — exclude sites from tracking, back up your data, or clear everything.
  • Refresh — saves whatever time has piled up right now instead of waiting for the next automatic save.
